This was a fantastic interview! (On the Long Thread podcast)

https://longthread.fireside.fm/s15e8

Sally Pointer is an experimental archaeologist, and while this discussion started with her work on nalbinding, which is an interesting craft all on its own, what grabbed me about the episode was her joy and excitement about how much we can learn about the people of the past by looking at their textile crafts. She has a beautiful way of looking at the crafts and the study of these crafts.

Nalbinding is one of those yarn-looping crafts that I have tried and thrown away in frustration before (knitting and crochet are in the same bag), but listening to this made me want to give it another go!
