I made this chicken and rice skillet, and it was delicious! A tad oily, probably because I used more oil than needed (I added more before sauteing the onions, which in hindsight I really didn't need to do). The chicken cooked through perfectly, and the rice and onions were delicious. I'd dice instead of slicing the onions next time.

https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/777998043-scallion-chicken-and-rice-for-two

The rice cooks to the bottom of the skillet, but the food has a lot of moisture so it will stay warm while you clean the pan. Hot water into the hot pan, scrape everything loose, and then wash and dry.

This one is a keeper!
