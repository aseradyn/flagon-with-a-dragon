# The Grand Canyon

Wow. Just ... wow. This place is really something to see. It's beautiful, and awesome, and there is soooo much to look at. Every part of the park gives you a different angle, exposing different colors and textures. I just couldn't get enough of it.

![A close-up of red and pink rock, eroded into smooth but complex curves, cut through by a narrow water channel (currently dry)](/photos/places/grand-canyon/PC220183.jpg "The sedimentary layers of the canyon are wildly different colors. Here, it was red, almost pink, but the water has begun to expose the pale brown layer beneath it.")
